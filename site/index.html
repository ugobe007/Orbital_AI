Digital Net · our own sensing hardware

See. Measure. Validate.
Real robot work.

Most physical-AI platforms borrow their eyes from phones, store CCTV, and the robots themselves. Orbital builds its own: a modular frame of 8–16 synchronized cameras that puts a 3-D digital net around a deployed robot, measures real-world performance, catches deviations, and records training-grade data.

Sensors

Synchronized, calibrated, ours

Global-shutter RGB cameras, depth cameras, and IR illumination on one PoE network with hardware time sync, so every frame from every angle lines up to the same instant.

Calibration

Known geometry, every time

AprilTag and ChArUco targets give the rig its geometry automatically, and the residuals say which camera moved — so the volume can be re-established on any floor.

Depth from many views

Stereo from RGB

Neighbouring cameras act as stereo pairs. Multi-view disparity recovers depth, shape, orientation, and motion of the robot and the objects it handles.

Output

3-D pose & trajectory

Precise motion tracking of the robot's body and arms, compared with what the robot reports about itself.

Output

Object interaction

Grasp, placement, cycle time, and success rate — measured from outside the robot, the same way for every OEM.

Output

Golden Run analytics

Record one good run of a job; every later run is compared with it to detect drift, deviations, and recoveries — and becomes labelled training data.
